    FAQ

    Common questions

    What is Tend?

    Tend is intake and prioritization software for hair salons, med spas, and dental practices in Sweden. It reads every intake form the moment it's submitted and arranges your day around what needs attention first.

    Does Tend replace my booking system?

    No. Tend sits alongside your existing booking tools and adds the intake and priority layer most salons, med spas, and clinics are missing.

    Is client data handled securely?

    Yes. All intake data is encrypted and stored within the EU, with access limited to your own team.

    How long does setup take?

    Most practices are running their first digital intake form within a few days, since forms and priority rules are configured rather than custom-built.
